Candidate maps

New maps built for room to manoeuvre: open ground for big formations, a few chokepoints, and cover a line abreast can be ambushed from. None of them is dealt by the random pick; play each one by name with the command on its card, then make your call. Your call is saved on this page, and Claude reads it.

The numbers on each card are measured from the map itself before anyone played it, and compared with maps you already know.

Version 4, built at 0ae17e29.

The Parade Ground

parade

March a whole squad line abreast across 112 m of open floor, and get shot down the length of that line by a squad waiting in the bay of container walls on either side. Or post your own squad in a bay, facing out at right angles to their advance, and do it to them. The slow way is round the back of a bay, past a neck.

Room
A line of four at its own spacing can drive through 65% of the field (the corridor maps you play: 7–8%; Foundry, which you cut: 88%). The widest line the field takes: 21 vehicles abreast.
Open, with ambush ground
From the middle you can see 82% of the field. The last maps this open (40–64%) you cut, and they had nowhere to hide; the maps you kept see 18–33%. What is new here: a line of four crossing the middle base to base can be shot down its length from cover it could not see when it set off, with room there for 19 hidden vehicles (6 in the best spot).
Formations
Measured with the game's own seating: in the middle, every formation fits, Line included; and in the bay on either side, a line of four stands between its two walls facing out over the floor. That is the ambush you described, at right angles to a line crossing.
The computer, playing it
In computer-against-computer matches about 31% of the vehicles pushed past the middle, and they spent 41% of their time on the open floor. Neither base won more by being on its side. It fights in the open and does not use the bays yet: they are past the range it looks for cover in on its own. You can post a squad there.
A squad on the move
Four tanks sent 150 m forward on an attack-move got there in about 14 s. On the Sumps they take about 40 s, and before tonight's fix to how squads hold formation in a tight lane they never got there at all. (4 test runs on the laptop.)
Scraping
Long vehicles scrape containers about 215 times a minute here; on the Container Yard you play, 794.
Chokepoints
8 narrow places on the main routes, the tightest 16 m of drivable width; 7 of them have a way round. Necks under 20 m: 16 m, 18 m.
make skirmish ARENA=parade
