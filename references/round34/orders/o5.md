   rectangle, and the hexagonal maps' corners are walls. Those squads stop at the wall and never read "arrived" (the

5. **Shuffle after "arrived" is large everywhere** (brains' B6): the worst crew moves 20–48 m after its squad

## How to run it

`make orders-probe` (headless matrix + table, `ORDERS_PROBE_ARMS` takes O2's switch when it lands, e.g.
`ORDERS_PROBE_ARMS="main: o2_off:--o2-flag=off"`), `make orders-probe-shots` (needs a display). One case by hand:
`godot --fixed-fps 30 --path . --script res://tests/orders_probe.gd -- --arena=locks --seed=34 --probe-squads=5
--probe-route=click [--ground-cap=off] [--halt-price=off] [--probe-trace=N] [--probe-shots=DIR]`. The probe pins
`--squad-cap` to its squad count (his laptop preset fields 4 of 5) and parks the one-scout enemy in the far corner.
